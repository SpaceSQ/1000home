🏛️ BRAND HERITAGE & VISION · FROM 2000 "QIANJIA ZHIKE" TO 2026 "1000HOME"

26 Years Dedicated to One Belief:
“Bringing Intelligent Living to A Thousand Homes.”

Founded in 2000, Guangzhou Smarthome Technology Co., Ltd. (广州智家科技有限公司) is a pioneering smart manufacturing consulting advisor, industry brand evaluation institution, and smart product buying-guide company. Guided since day one by our founding mission—“Bringing Intelligent Living to Thousands of Households (将智能化生活带给千家万户)”—we built the renowned industry service brand Qianjia Zhike (千家智客 / 千家网), literally meaning “A Thousand Homes Smart Advisor.”

Over the past 26 years, through our proprietary Qianjia Brand Index (千家品牌指数), the Qianjia Brand Lab (千家品牌实验室), the China International Building Intelligence Summit (中国国际建筑智能化峰会), and our Smart Supply Chain Advisory (智能建筑供应链顾问), we have evaluated thousands of global smart home, architectural lighting, HVAC, security, and robotics manufacturers—serving as the trusted bridge between world-class intelligent manufacturing and modern residential spaces.

In 2026, marking our 26th anniversary, Guangzhou Smarthome Technology Co., Ltd. proudly launches www.1000home.com—the global, AI-native evolution of “千家 (1000Home).” Combining 26 years of deep supply-chain vetting with our proprietary 4-Slot Reasoning Funnel Dialog and shared on-device LLM engine (group.com.robotzero.shared.ai), 1000Home reimagines residential product discovery for North American families through our “Eight Titans (八大金刚)” Mystery Shopping App Matrix and our AI Agent Data Protocol.

✨ THE MEANING OF "1000HOME" (千家之源)

In Chinese, “千家 (Qianjia)” represents “Thousands of Households (千家万户).” From launching qianjia.com in 2000 to unveiling 1000home.com in 2026, our name carries a quarter-century commitment: demystifying complex smart hardware and turning every home into an effortless, inspiring sanctuary.
